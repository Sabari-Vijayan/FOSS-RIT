About the Project
Interview Helper is an AI-powered interview preparation platform that helps users practice technical interview questions, receive AI-based feedback on their answers, and generate questions based on their resume.

Users can generate interview questions by topic and difficulty, submit answers for AI-based evaluation, upload a resume PDF to generate tailored questions, save previous interview sessions, and manage their sessions using Supabase authentication.

The project uses React and Vite for the frontend, Node.js and Express for the backend, Groq API for AI functionality, and Supabase for authentication and data management.
